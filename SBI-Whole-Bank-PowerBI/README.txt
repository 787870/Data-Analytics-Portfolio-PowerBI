SBI Whole Bank — Power BI pack
Unofficial analytical desk from published State Bank of India results.
Not affiliated with SBI or Microsoft.

HOW TO OPEN
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open SBIBank.pbip in Power BI Desktop (version from 2025 or later).
   If Desktop asks you to enable the Power BI enhanced report format (PBIR), accept.
   The Executive page opens with FY26 KPI cards, net profit by year, deposit mix and quarterly profit.
   All data is embedded in the model. No gateway and no sign-in.
3. Or, in Power BI Desktop: Home > Get data > Excel workbook > SBI-Whole-Bank.xlsx.
   Select Annual, Quarters, Mix, Circles and Snapshot, then Load.
   Suggested first visuals: column chart of NetProfitCr by FiscalYear, donut of Mix where Group = Deposits, card of GNPA.

Sheets
- Annual       one row per fiscal year, FY24 to FY26
- Quarters     reported quarter-end points
- Mix          deposit and advance composition
- Circles      illustrative FY26 circle split (not official MIS)
- Snapshot     30 Jun 2026 published markers and FY26 extras
- DAX          measures you can paste if you build from Excel

Ratio columns in Excel are real percentages.
Circle deposits and advances add back to the published FY26 whole-bank totals.
